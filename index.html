Our Mission

A Lifeline
Across Borders

SLSUR exists to ensure that no Sierra Leonean student faces the challenges of life in Russia alone. From navigating bureaucracy to building friendships across thousands of kilometres from home, we stand as a bridge between two worlds.

We advocate for student welfare, organise cultural celebrations, and create pathways for academic and professional growth — all while keeping the spirit of Sierra Leone alive in the Russian Heartland.
